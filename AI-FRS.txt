Yes. That actually gives us a **cleaner and more focused project**.

We will stop the pipeline at **video screening**. There will be no separate coding test, aptitude test, or interview module.

The final recruitment decision will be based on:

**Candidate Profile + Resume + Resume Ranking + Video Screening → Final Ranking → Recruited Candidates**

### Therefore, before we continue with `CGPA`, I would now freeze our data architecture as:

```text
1. USERS
   └── Authentication

2. CANDIDATES
   └── Candidate profile

3. JOBS
   └── Job + number of openings + requirements

4. APPLICATIONS
   └── Candidate applies to job

5. RESUMES
   └── Uploaded resume + extracted information

6. RESUME_RANKING

   └── Audio + NLP + CV features + video score

   └── Final score + rank + recruited status
```

**This is the architecture I'd lock in now.**

Then we can return to our synthetic candidate generator and continue with **academic information → skills → projects → internships → certifications**, knowing exactly where every piece of data will eventually be used.
